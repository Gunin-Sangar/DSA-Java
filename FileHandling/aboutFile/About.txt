- Streams are of 2 types
    Byte Stream           [InputStream and OutputStream] [both ends in "Stream"]
    Character Stream      [Reader, Writer] [ends with "Read" or "Write"]
    [both "Abstract" classes and also extends Object directly]


- BYTE STREAMS
    InputStream [implements Closeable(which extends AutoCloseable) both interfaces with void close() method only]
    - Direct Known Subclasses:
      AudioInputStream, ByteArrayInputStream, FileInputStream, FilterInputStream,
      InputStream, ObjectInputStream, PipedInputStream, SequenceInputStream, StringBufferInputStream

    OutputStream [implements Closeable(which extends AutoCloseable) both interfaces with void close() method only]
    - Direct Known Subclasses:
      ByteArrayOutputStream, FileOutputStream, FilterOutputStream, ObjectOutputStream, OutputStream, PipedOutputStream
